Implement `nms_xyxy(boxes, scores, threshold)`. Convert boxes/scores to float64. Require boxes shape `(n,4)` with finite ordered corners `(x1,y1,x2,y2)`, scores finite shape `(n,)`, and finite threshold in [0,1]; otherwise raise ValueError. Greedily keep boxes by descending score, breaking ties by lower original index. Suppress a later box only when IoU is strictly greater than threshold; zero-area boxes have IoU 0. Return kept original indices as int64 in keep order.
