Implement parse_env_lines(text). Ignore blank lines and lines whose stripped form begins with #. For each other line, strip surrounding whitespace from the whole line, split at the first '=', and require a key matching [A-Z_][A-Z0-9_]*. Preserve the value exactly after the first '='. For duplicate keys keep the first occurrence. Raise ValueError for a non-comment line without '=' or with an invalid key.
