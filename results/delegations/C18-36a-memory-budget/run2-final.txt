## GDN formula and peak

One recurrent state is `M = 1 × 32 × 128 × 128 × 4 = 2,097,152 B` (2 MiB). The budget counts four state-sized slots per timestep—recurrent state, decay product, state/key product, and key/delta update product—so **`4 × T × M = 8,388,608 × T B`, or 8 MiB/token**. At T=1000, that term is 8,388,608,000 B (7.8125 GiB). The chunk-boundary term is separate: `(ceil(T/64)+1) × M`.

The traced ops loop and checkpoint VJP explain why the budget does not reduce backward memory to 64 states. Exact simultaneous buffer lifetimes remain unproven; the query product and other VJP arrays may change overlap. This source-derived model predicts 26.514 GiB active peak at backward replay of layer 38. Its three largest co-live terms are:

1. Resident weights: **19,508,787,456 B (18.169 GiB)**
2. GDN state arrays: **8,388,608,000 B (7.813 GiB)**
3. GDN projection activations: **110,976,000 B (0.103 GiB)**
